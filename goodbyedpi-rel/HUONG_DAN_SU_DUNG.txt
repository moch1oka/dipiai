================================================================================
          HUONG DAN SU DUNG GOODBYEDPI - VIETNAM OPTIMIZED
================================================================================

1. LUA CHON KICH BAN
--------------------
- service_install_vietnam.cmd:
  Cai dat GoodbyeDPI thanh Windows Service tu khoi dong cung he thong.
  Ap dung cho cac domain trong vietnam-blacklist.txt.

- 1_vietnam_optimized.cmd:
  Chay truc tiep trong cua so console voi vietnam-blacklist.txt.
  Can giu cua so mo trong suot qua trinh su dung.

- 2_vietnam_all_traffic.cmd / service_install_vietnam_all.cmd:
  Xu ly toan bo luu luong (khong loc theo blacklist).

- dns_quick_switch.cmd:
  Doi nhanh DNS may tinh (IPv4 + IPv6) sang Cloudflare hoac Google.

- service_remove.cmd:
  Dung va go bo hoan toan service va driver WinDivert.


2. CAU HINH SECURE DNS (DoH)
----------------------------
Can bat DNS bao mat tren trinh duyet de tranh bi chan o tang DNS:
- Chrome / Edge / Brave / Coc Coc:
  Cai dat -> Quyen rieng tu & Bao mat -> Bao mat -> Bat "Su dung DNS an toan"
  -> Chon nha cung cap (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 hoac Google 8.8.8.8).
- Firefox:
  Cai dat -> Cai dat mang -> Bat DNS over HTTPS -> Chon Cloudflare.


3. QUAN LY BLACKLIST
--------------------
- Cap nhat tu dong: Chay update_blacklist.cmd.
- Them thu cong:
  Mo file vietnam-blacklist.txt va them domain vao danh sach (moi dong 1 domain).
  Neu dang chay service, khoi dong lai service de ap dung thay doi.
